# Governance

## Project Ownership
Ohverlay is maintained by Michael Futol (FutolTech Engineering & Project Systems). As the primary author and architect, Michael Futol holds final decision-making authority over the repository, its technical direction, and its governance.

## Maintainer Authority
The maintainer has the final say on:
- Technical design and architecture decisions
- Merging pull requests
- Setting the release schedule and cutting release tags
- Approving changes to the security and privacy policies
- Licensing modifications
- Ecosystem and marketplace strategy

## Contributor Expectations
We welcome external collaboration. Contributors are expected to:
- Adhere to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Follow the guidelines established in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Respect the ethical boundaries regarding user privacy (no surveillance, no silent monitoring).
- Ensure that any proposed features align with the core product doctrine (Technical Office, Living Overlays, Blue AI, etc.).

## Future Community Participation
As the project matures and transitions from a pre-release state into a broader public offering, this governance model may evolve to include structured contributor roles, technical steering committees, or formalized marketplace creator programs. Until such a transition occurs, all authority remains centralized with the principal maintainer.

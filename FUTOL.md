## 🌟 Our Story

**Futol Ethical Technology Ecosystems** was founded on a simple belief: *technology should serve humanity, not exploit it.*

In an age of attention-economy apps and data-hungry platforms, we choose a different path. We build software that:
- Respects your attention and time
- Protects your privacy by design
- Promotes mental wellness
- Works sustainably on modest hardware

## 🏛️ Core Principles

### 1️⃣ Privacy First
```
├── Local processing by default
├── No data collection without explicit consent
├── Transparent data practices
└── User owns their data
```

### 2️⃣ Calm Technology
```
├── Non-intrusive design
├── No addictive patterns
├── Respects user's attention
└── Calm-by-default UX
```

### 3️⃣ Ethical AI
```
├── Transparent AI decision-making
├── Human-in-the-loop for important choices
├── Bias-aware development
└── Explainable systems
```

### 4️⃣ Sustainability
```
├── Low resource footprint
├── Works on older hardware
├── Energy efficient
└── Long-lasting software
```

### 5️⃣ Accessibility
```
├── Affordable (often free)
├── Works offline
├── Inclusive design
└── Global availability
```

Privacy & Ethical Design Commitments

Ohverlay is committed to protecting user autonomy, privacy, and digital peace of mind.

1. Zero Telemetry & Silent Tracking

Ohverlay contains no analytics beacons, tracking scripts, or usage telemetry. We do not collect, store, or transmit your computer's activity, open applications, or user habits.

2. Local Configuration Storage

Your preferences remain 100% on your device at `~/.ohverlay/config.json`. No external user accounts or cloud authentication servers are required.

3. Transparent & Open Source

Our entire desktop application codebase is open source under the MIT License, allowing anyone to inspect, build, and verify our privacy guarantees directly.

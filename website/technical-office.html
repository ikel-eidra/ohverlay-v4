Technical Office Origins

Ohverlay did not start as a screensaver or aesthetic decoration. It originated inside a real, high-demand Saudi Arabian engineering office where technical teams needed a unobtrusive way to deliver urgent task instructions, track supervisor approvals, and hand off local files without interrupting active CAD and structural engineering applications.

Historical Capabilities (Saudi-Era Prototype)

  • Sticky Instructions & Timed Tasks: Floating persistent note overlays anchored to desktop corners with countdown deadlines.
  • Staff Acknowledgements: Interactive receipt buttons confirming task execution back to supervisor dashboards.
  • Local-Network File Handoffs: Direct peer-to-peer file transfer between workstation overlays on the local LAN.
  • Supervisor Workstation Dashboard: Centralized real-time overview of active office task statuses.

Current Status & Planned Rebuild

The current Ohverlay Desktop Runtime (`python main.py`) focuses on a clean, stable nature baseline. The Technical Office capabilities are scheduled for a complete modular rebuild under our privacy-conscious local-first architecture.

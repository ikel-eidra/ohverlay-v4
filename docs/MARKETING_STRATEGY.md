# 🚀 Ohverlay Marketing & Growth Strategy (Free Version)

This strategy outlines how to market **Ohverlay** as a free, open-source ambient desktop application to build high-volume user adoption, community trust, and product-led growth (PLG) without ad spend.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience Personas

1. **Desk Setup & Aesthetic Enthusiasts:** Users who spend hours curating wallpapers (Wallpaper Engine, Rainmeter, custom taskbars).
2. **Lofi & Chillhop Streamers / Viewers:** People who enjoy relaxing backgrounds, ambient study setups, and cozy coding environments.
3. **Developers & WFH Professionals:** People who want non-intrusive, serene movement on their second monitor or coding background.
4. **Productivity & Digital Wellness Seekers:** Users looking for subtle stress reduction and mindful visual rests during long work sessions.

---

## 📣 Channel-by-Channel Launch Playbook

### 1. Short-Form Aesthetic Video Marketing (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
* **Format:** 10–15 second videos showing a mechanical keyboard, dark VS Code/IDE setup, cozy lighting, and Ohverlay fireflies/dragonflies drifting across the monitor screen.
* **Audio:** Soft lofi beat or ambient rain sounds.
* **Text Overlay:** *"Added floating fireflies to my Windows desk setup ✨ (Free & 100% offline)"*
* **Call to Action:** *"Link in bio to download free portable ZIP."*

### 2. Community Showcase (Reddit)
* **Subreddits:**
  - `r/DesktopEnhancements`
  - `r/windows`
  - `r/selfhosted`
  - `r/Productivity`
  - `r/Workspaces`
* **Post Title Example:** *"I built a free, zero-CPU desktop overlay that adds transparent floating fireflies and dandelions to Windows [Open Source]"*
* **Content:** Include a 15-second clear GIF/video preview and direct links to the GitHub repo and landing page.

### 3. Developer & Tech Community Launch
* **Hacker News (Show HN):**
  - Title: *Show HN: Ohverlay – Ultra-lightweight transparent desktop nature overlays in PySide6*
  - Focus: Privacy-first, zero telemetry, local Qt WebEngine canvas rendering, click-through window flags.
* **Product Hunt Launch:**
  - Tagline: *Transform your Windows desktop into a serene living ecosystem.*
* **Itch.io Release:**
  - Publish Ohverlay under the **Tools / Utility** section on Itch.io for instant free downloads and community reviews.

---

## 📈 Product-Led Growth (PLG) & Virality Loops

1. **Portable ZIP (No Setup Required):** Zero friction download. Users don't need admin rights or installer prompts—just extract and double click.
2. **Social Screenshot Hotkey:** Add a easy shortcut or Control Center button to save a clean screenshot of the user's desktop with ambient overlays for sharing on Twitter/X (`#DeskSetup`).
3. **Open Source & Transparency:** 100% offline security builds trust on Reddit and Hacker News.

---

## 🗓️ 4-Week Execution Checklist

- [x] **Week 1:** Launch Landing Page (`website/index.html`) & Portable ZIP (`Ohverlay-Portable.zip`).
- [ ] **Week 2:** Publish Show HN & Product Hunt release.
- [ ] **Week 3:** Share aesthetic desk setup clips on TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts.
- [ ] **Week 4:** Post showcase on Reddit (`r/DesktopEnhancements`, `r/Workspaces`).

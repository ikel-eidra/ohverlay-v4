# Product Doctrine

Ohverlay is a comprehensive desktop ecosystem built around six core product lanes. All features must map to one of these lanes.

## 1. Technical Office
**Purpose:** Professional coordination and local communication.
- Multiple draggable, editable sticky notes.
- Countdown and elapsed-time timers.
- Deadlines and sender/receiver tracking.
- Opened/acknowledged status reporting.
- Staff-to-supervisor reporting and instruction workflows.
- Local-network or shared-folder communication (no cloud dependency required).
- Standard User and Supervisor roles with dashboards.
- Explicit permission and consent controls.

## 2. Desktop Living Overlays
**Purpose:** Ambient, non-distracting environmental life.
- Nature and ambient overlays: Creatures, plants, weather.
- Taskbar-scale living objects (e.g., Dragonflies, Dandelions, Fireflies).
- Aquarium creatures and environmental effects.
- Fully click-through, always-on-top, transparent execution.
- Multi-monitor support.
- Strict requirement for ultra-low CPU and RAM footprint.

## 3. Productivity Overlays
**Purpose:** Workspace enhancement and time management.
- Standard sticky notes, timers, and reminders.
- Break prompts and schedule notifications.
- Study aids and exam overlays (e.g., PlumberPass).
- Small AI-powered workspace utilities.

## 4. Blue AI
**Purpose:** Ethical, user-controlled artificial intelligence.
- Optional AI assistant functionality.
- Local-first memory where practical.
- User-controlled screen awareness (Blue Vision).
- **Privacy Core:** Explicit consent required for screenshots or visual analysis. No silent monitoring. No employer surveillance without employee knowledge and consent.

## 5. Marketplace
**Purpose:** Distribution and extensibility.
- Central catalog for discovering overlays.
- Support for free and future paid overlay packs.
- User-installed overlays with versioning.
- UI for previewing, installing, enabling, disabling, updating, and uninstalling.
- Future support for creator entitlements.

## 6. Future OHVER Ecosystem
**Purpose:** Community participation and rewards (Future Architecture).
- Internal entitlement/credits architecture based on the "OHVER" concept.
- Proposed fixed internal supply of 1,000,000.
- Focused on ethical utility (creator rewards, promotional redemptions) rather than speculative hype.
- **Constraints:** No smart contracts, transferable tokens, presales, exchange listings, or crypto payment flows at this time.
